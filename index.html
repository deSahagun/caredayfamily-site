1 in 5medical appointments are missed

A study of ten outpatient clinics found that 18.8% of appointments were no-shows, and each one cost about $196 in 2008 dollars. Most people don't skip on purpose. The date slips by, a school pickup runs late, or the appointment was booked months ago.

Reminders help. In a review of 21 studies, patients who got digital reminders were about 25% less likely to miss their appointment.

Booked months ahead

Six months is a long time to remember a Wednesday.

Dentist cleanings, annual physicals and specialist follow-ups are often booked half a year out. By the time the date comes around, the reminder card is long gone.

Add the appointment the day you book it, and CareDay Family keeps track of it until it's time to go.
